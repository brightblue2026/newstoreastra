# Estimated parcel weights (14 October 2026)

Shiprocket rates every parcel on the larger of its real weight and its box
volume. No MX-3 size had a weight or box saved, so every order would have been
charged as the default 20 × 15 × 15 cm carton. At the owner's request these
estimates were saved on every MX-3 bottle variant on production (74 variants);
each change is in the audit log.

| Size   | Packed weight | Box (L × B × H) |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| 225 ml | 300 g         | 8 × 5 × 18 cm   |
| 450 ml | 550 g         | 9 × 6 × 22 cm   |
| 900 ml | 1,050 g       | 11 × 7 × 27 cm  |

They are estimates: liquid at about 1 g per ml, plus the bottle and packing.
Weigh and measure one packed bottle of each size, then enter the real figures in
each variant's form in the admin (Products → Variants → Edit). The 3 L cans have
no estimate and are not on sale.
