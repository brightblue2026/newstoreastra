# India ecommerce compliance review — 13 October 2026

This is a technical gap review for Bright Blue's own-brand Indian store, not a legal opinion, licence verification or compliance certificate. Have Indian ecommerce/privacy counsel and the business's accountant confirm applicability, seller structure, current notifications and real business facts before launch. Do not assume the manufacturing company, trading seller and payment-account holder are interchangeable.

## Current sources and timing

1. [Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/files/E%20commerce%20rules_1732703966.pdf), especially rule 4: grievance acknowledgement within 48 hours and redress within one month, with affirmative purchase consent. The 2021 amendment also needs to be read for the relevant entity structure. A support inbox alone does not establish an appointed grievance officer. The policy wording was corrected from “30 days” to “one month”; a 48-hour damaged-parcel reporting preference now expressly preserves statutory rights.
2. [Department of Consumer Affairs latest notifications](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/latest-news) lists a September 2026 ecommerce amendment and an October 2026 packaged-declarations advisory. [The 2026 amendment](https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/public/upload/admin/cmsfiles/whatsnews/E_Commerce_Amendment_Rules_2026_2026-09-11_18-12-58.pdf) includes complaint-copy, imported-origin, sponsored-listing, prior-price and dark-pattern provisions. Visual inspection of Gazette page 5 confirms commencement on **1 January 2027** (G.S.R. 789(E), 9 September 2026). Treat these as upcoming requirements, not already-operative amendments on 13 October 2026. Plan prior-price history and the other applicable changes before that date. No unsupported compliance badge or self-audit certificate has been published.
3. [Kerala Legal Metrology rules](https://lmd.kerala.gov.in/2025/06/09/relevant-rules/), rule 6(10), addresses online mandatory declarations. Check later national amendments as well, especially if importing goods.
4. [FSSAI Labelling and Display compendium](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Comp_Labelling%20Display_Version%20VIII_09_09_2025.pdf), regulation 4(2), requires applicable label information before an online food sale, with specified batch/date exceptions. This source does not prove Bright Blue's licences, product safety, shelf-life practices or compliance with later amendments.
5. [DPDP commencement notification G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) stages provisions at publication, one year and eighteen months from Gazette publication. [Rules G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) likewise stages rules. As of this review, do not treat all substantive duties/rights as already commenced; prepare now and have counsel calendar the exact applicable dates. [October 2026 removal-of-difficulties order](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/10/daf495a7b5798a3dfa72a944b51fc176.pdf) corrects guardianship/data-audit wording; it does not make this store automatically compliant.
6. [CBIC, CGST Act section 36](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000306/1000001): covered records generally require 72 months from the annual-return due date for that year, with longer preservation in specified proceedings. This is not a reason to retain all customer/marketing data indefinitely.

## Evidence required from the business

Confirm the seller-of-record entity and payment KYC; provide approved GST/FSSAI/other applicable licences, the appointed grievance officer's contact details, actual product labels, approved returns/delivery commitments, documented retention and breach procedures, and invoice approval. Policy acceptance is now enforced at the submission boundary; an immutable per-order policy-version/consent audit record remains future work. No legal facts have been manufactured to fill gaps.
